Privacy policy

This policy applies to every application listed under Personal apps.

  • Personal use. These apps are used only by their owner, to access the owner's own accounts. They are not offered to other users.
  • No data collection. The apps do not collect, store on third-party servers, sell, or share any user data.
  • Where data goes. Data accessed through a provider's API (for example, files in Google Drive) is transferred only between that provider and the owner's own devices.
  • Credentials. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's devices and can be revoked at any time from the provider's account settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
